@H2{Challenge} @BIGP{Oqto had built its product without ever having a designer. They needed a design system clear enough to use on their own afterwards, so the product stays consistent as it grows. The design also had to meet contrast requirements, fix the most obvious UX problems and reflect Oqto’s brand, on the web and in the app.}
@STEP1{01 Foundation|Colours and typography|The system starts small. Sand, Mist, Orange and Ink carry the whole product and give it a look you don’t expect from an accounting service. Sandor is the primary typeface, with Geist Mono in capitals for labels.}
