Tink

I joined Tink as the only designer in a team of ten. Six years later it had more than 550 people and was Europe’s leading open banking platform.

Challenge

Everything was still to build: a personal finance app, a brand, and a way to keep it all consistent as the company grew fast.

Process

01 Identity

The foundation: logo, colour and form

The logo and colours were developed in close collaboration with the branding agency Kurppa Hosk. I then took the identity further and brought it into the product, marketing assets and sales materials.

02 Design system

From identity to interface

I built Tink’s design system and helped hire and mentor the designers and developers who used it, so that a growing team could ship consistent work on every platform.

03 The app

The system in the product

I designed Tink’s personal finance app for iOS and Android. As Tink moved into open banking, my focus shifted to the new products, including the user flows for payments and data aggregation, working with clients such as American Express, NatWest, ABN Amro and Nordea.

Outcome

An award-winning app, a brand and a design system that scaled with the company, and open banking flows used by millions across Europe.
