Services

Four ways I can help.

New product or feature

From a rough idea to a product people can use. I design flows, interfaces and prototypes, and work alongside you the whole way.

Design systems

A system your team actually uses. Tokens, components and guidelines that AI tools such as Claude Code can build from.

Product redesign

When the product has outgrown its design. I review what is there, prioritise what makes the biggest difference and improve it step by step.

Brand and identity

Logo, colours and typography, all the way to a full visual identity with clear guidelines. On its own, or together with the product work.

About

For more than 15 years, I’ve designed digital products – from agency work for SAS and ICA to being one of Tink’s first employees and leading new products at Klarna.

I care about the details and stay involved until the product works in real users’ hands. AI is an everyday tool for me, whether I’m designing or building.

Today I freelance from Åre in northern Sweden, working with teams remotely or on site, full or part time.
