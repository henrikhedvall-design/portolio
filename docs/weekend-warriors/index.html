Weekend Warriors

Lettering, identity and animated titles for a ski film about fitting a whole winter into the weekends.

Idea

Friends who used to ski all winter. With jobs and families, they are now down to a few weekends a year. The graphics follow the mood of the film: hand-lettered titles and a countdown to the next weekend on skis.
